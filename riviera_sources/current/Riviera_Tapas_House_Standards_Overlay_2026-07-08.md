### Chorizo and Mozzarella Arancini

- Recipe ID: `arancini`
- Yield: 80 large arancini @ 100 g
- Label: Arancini
- Tags: House Standard \| Canape \| Canape / Tapas \| Deep Fry \| Gluten-Free \| pork \| dairy \| Weddings \| Baby Shower
- Package / section refs: Weddings: Portofino Package; Weddings: Taormina Package; Weddings: Late Night Snacks; Baby Shower: High Tea
- Menu description: Lemon & Thyme Aioli · Romesco · Microherbs
- Elements: Arancini (3 per serve) \| Romesco \| Lemon Thyme Aioli \| Microherbs

#### Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient / prep |
| --- | --- |
| 5 kg | Arborio Rice |
| 1 kg | Chorizo - finely diced |
| 600 g | Mozzarella — Whole - grated |
| 400 g | Brown Onion - blitzed in food processor |
| 5 L | Water |
| 200 ml | Chicken Stock Concentrate |
| 600 g | Cheddar Cheese — Whole Vintage - grated |
| 150 g | Parmesan — Grana Padano - grated |
| 800 g | Spicy Capsicum Dip |
| 1 kg | Roasted Capsicum - drained and blitzed |
| 750 ml | White Wine |
| 200 g | Garlic — Crushed |
| 50 g | Salt Flakes - plus extra to finish |
| 30 g | Cracked Pepper |
| 300 g | GF Flour - for coating |
| 800 g | GF Breadcrumbs - for coating |
| 10 | Eggs - beaten for coating |

#### Prep method

1. PREP AROMATICS: Finely dice or blitz the brown onion. Sauté in a large pot over medium heat until golden and soft.
2. CHORIZO BASE: Add crushed garlic and finely diced chorizo. Cook 1–2 minutes until the chorizo browns and releases its fat. Deglaze with white wine and reduce briefly.
3. RICE MIX: Add Arborio rice, spicy capsicum dip, onion, garlic and chorizo to a deep bain. Add water, chicken stock concentrate, salt and pepper. Stir until evenly mixed.
4. COOK: Cover the bain with a lid or foil. Bake at 160°C for 20 minutes, stir, then bake another 20 minutes or until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is cooked.
5. COOL: Transfer the rice mixture to shallow bains and chill completely. Use the freezer briefly if needed, but do not roll warm rice.
6. FINISH MIX: Grate the cheeses. Fold mozzarella, cheddar, parmesan and blitzed roasted capsicum through the cold rice until evenly incorporated. Taste and adjust seasoning.
7. PORTION: Weigh rice mix into 100 g portions and roll into tight balls.
8. CRUMB: Set up GF flour, beaten egg and GF breadcrumbs. Flour each ball, dip in egg, then coat thoroughly in breadcrumbs.
9. FREEZE & PACK: Freeze on lined trays until solid. Transfer to vacuum bags and SEAL ONLY — do not vacuum, or the arancini will squash.
10. DEFROST: Defrost in the coldroom for at least 24 hours before frying.

#### On the day / service

1. Preheat fryer to 180°C. Fry defrosted arancini until deep golden and hot through; check one with a skewer for centre heat.
2. Drain and season immediately with salt flakes.
3. Warm Romesco in a pot or microwave until hot.
4. Plate 60–80 ml warm Romesco as the base. Add 3 arancini per serve.
5. Dot Lemon Thyme Aioli on top of each arancini and finish with freshly cut microherbs.

#### Notes

New house spec from Tapas Canape Recipe Cards.docx. Large format is 100 g per arancini, 3 per serve. Seal frozen arancini bags only; do not vacuum.

### Calamari Fritti

- Recipe ID: `calamari`
- Yield: Batch: 2 kg squid strips + 1 kg baby octopus
- Label: Calamari
- Tags: House Standard \| Canape \| Canape / Tapas \| Deep Fry \| Gluten-Free \| seafood \| Weddings \| Parties
- Package / section refs: Weddings: Portofino Package; Weddings: Taormina Package; Weddings: Late Night Snacks; Parties: Crowd Pleaser Platters
- Menu description: Lemon Dill Aioli · Crispy Capers
- Elements: Calamari \| Flour Mix \| Lemon Dill Aioli \| Crispy Capers

#### Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient / prep |
| --- | --- |
| 2 kg | Pineapple Cut Squid Strips — Tenderised - defrosted |
| 1 kg | Baby Octopus |
| 500 g | Rice Flour |
| 100 g | GF Corn Flour |
| 20 g | Salt - plus extra for finishing |
| 10 g | Cracked Pepper - for flour mix |
| 100 g | Lemon Pepper Seasoning |
| 500 g | Baby Capers - drained thoroughly |

#### Prep method

1. PORTION SEAFOOD: When calamari and octopus come into stock, break them down straight away. Portion 15 calamari pieces and 3 baby octopus into each 500 ml PC, label and freeze in crates.
2. FLOUR MIX: Combine rice flour, GF corn flour, salt, cracked pepper and lemon pepper seasoning. Mix thoroughly and transfer to labelled PCs. Keep chilled for service.
3. CAPERS: Drain capers thoroughly and pat dry with chux or paper towel. Store in a PC lined with clean chux, label and refrigerate.

#### On the day / service

1. Preheat fryer to 190°C.
2. Fry capers for 30–60 seconds until crisp. Drain on chux and keep uncovered.
3. Coat calamari strips and baby octopus evenly in flour mix. Shake off excess flour.
4. Fry immediately in small batches until crisp and cooked through, about 1 minute for calamari and 1–2 minutes for baby octopus.
5. Season hot seafood with a pinch of salt.
6. TAPAS: Spoon 30–40 ml Lemon Dill Aioli onto the plate. Arrange fried calamari and baby octopus on top. Scatter crispy capers and microgreens.
7. CANAPE: Place a small dot of Lemon Dill Aioli in a bamboo boat, add calamari, and finish with crispy capers.

#### Notes

Keep capers very dry before frying. Wet capers spit badly and do not crisp.

### Kilpatrick Oyster

- Recipe ID: `oysters-kilpatrick`
- Yield: Kilpatrick sauce batch for approx. 40–50 oysters
- Label: Oysters Kilpatrick
- Tags: House Standard \| Canape \| Bake \| Gluten-Free \| seafood \| pork \| Weddings
- Package / section refs: Weddings: Portofino Package; Weddings: Taormina Package
- Menu description: Crispy Speck · Parsley · Lemon
- Elements: Oysters \| Diced Speck \| Kilpatrick Sauce \| Rock Salt \| Lemon \| Parsley

#### Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient / prep |
| --- | --- |
| as needed | Oysters - 1 per guest; defrost 24 hours in coldroom |
| 500 g | Speck - finely diced |
| 250 ml | GF Worcestershire Sauce |
| 60 ml | BBQ Sauce - converted from 1/4 cup |
| 30 g | Brown Sugar - approx. 2 tbsp, packed |
| 5 ml | Tabasco - converted from 1 tsp; adjust to taste |
| 5 g | Dried Parsley - converted from 2 tbsp |
| 2 kg | Rock Salt - for serving tray |
| 600 g | Lemons - approx. 4 lemons, sliced |
| 30 g | Fresh Parsley - chopped for garnish |

#### Prep method

1. OYSTERS: Remove oysters from freezer at least 24 hours before the event. Defrost in the coldroom, drain and keep chilled.
2. SPECK: Finely dice speck, place in a labelled PC and refrigerate.
3. KILPATRICK SAUCE: Whisk the brown sugar into the GF Worcestershire until fully dissolved, then mix in BBQ sauce, Tabasco and dried parsley. Transfer to a labelled squeeze bottle.
4. GARNISH: Slice lemons and chop parsley. Store separately in labelled PCs in the coldroom.

#### On the day / service

1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
2. Scrunch aluminium foil into a bain tray so oyster shells sit level and stable.
3. Arrange oysters on the foil. Add 5–8 ml Kilpatrick sauce to each oyster and top with 1 teaspoon diced speck.
4. Bake for about 10 minutes, until the speck is golden and the oyster is just warmed through.
5. Spread rock salt 2 cm thick on the serving tray. Nestle oysters into the salt and garnish with parsley, rocket or microgreens and lemon slices.

#### Notes

Bake hot and fast enough to render the speck without shrinking the oysters.

### Slow Cooked Veal Meatballs

- Recipe ID: `veal-meatballs`
- Yield: Approx. 90 balls @ 80 g; 50 tapas serves
- Label: Meatballs
- Tags: House Standard \| Tapas \| Slow Bake \| Gluten-Free \| veal \| dairy \| Weddings \| Riviera Table / Offsite
- Package / section refs: Weddings: Portofino Package; Weddings: Taormina Package; Weddings: Amalfi Package; Weddings: La Tavola Package; Riviera Table / Offsite: Canape Packages; Riviera Table / Offsite: Plated Meals; Riviera Table / Offsite: Feasting — Shared to Table
- Menu description: Romesco Sugo · Toasted Focaccia · Gremolata
- Elements: Meatballs \| Romesco Sugo \| Focaccia \| Gremolata \| Manchego

#### Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient / prep |
| --- | --- |
| 5 kg | Veal Mince |
| 600 g | GF Breadcrumbs |
| 450 g | GF Bread Loaf - approx. 3/4 loaf, blitzed into small chunks |
| 600 g | Brown Onion - approx. 4 medium onions, blitzed |
| 100 g | Garlic Cloves - approx. 20 cloves, blitzed |
| 400 g | Parmesan Cheese - blitzed |
| 500 ml | Thickened Cream |
| 20 g | Flat Leaf Italian Parsley - finely chopped; converted from 1/2 cup |
| 15 g | Basil - finely chopped; converted from 1/2 cup |
| 15 g | Oregano - finely chopped; converted from 1/2 cup |
| 5 g | Mixed Italian Herbs — Dried - converted from 1 tbsp |
| 30 g | Salt - converted from 2 tbsp; adjust after test cook |
| 30 g | Cracked Pepper - adjust after test cook |
| 5 | Eggs |
| as needed | Romesco Sugo - enough to cover meatballs in baking bain |
| as needed | Focaccia - 3 slices per serve, 2 cm thick |
| as needed | Basic Gremolata - parsley, lemon zest and garlic |
| as needed | Manchego - finely grated to finish |

#### Prep method

1. GREMOLATA: Prepare basic gremolata with parsley, lemon zest and garlic. Hold chilled and use same day.
2. MIX: Blitz onion and garlic in a food processor. Mix through the breadcrumbs so the onion juices wet the crumbs. Add this breadcrumb mix to the veal mince with blitzed GF bread, parmesan, cream, herbs, salt, pepper and eggs.
3. TEST: Cook a small patty and adjust salt, pepper and herb balance before rolling the full batch.
4. SHAPE: Wet hands to prevent sticking. Weigh into 80 g balls and roll firmly.
5. BAKE: Place meatballs in a deep bain and cover generously with Romesco Sugo. Bake covered at 150°C for 1 hour, or until cooked through and tender.
6. COOL & STORE: Cool meatballs in the sauce. Portion into PCs in serves of 3, distributing sauce evenly. Label and freeze in the meatballs crate.

#### On the day / service

1. Reheat meatballs and sauce in the microwave or oven until steaming hot throughout, minimum 75°C internal.
2. Serve 3 meatballs with sauce in a medium serving bowl.
3. Toast 3 slices of focaccia, 2 cm thick, until golden on both sides.
4. Finish meatballs with finely grated Manchego and gremolata. Serve focaccia alongside.

#### Notes

New DOCX spec returns this recipe to veal mince and 80 g balls. Prep time guideline: about 45 minutes per 90 balls once mise is ready.

### Limoncello Chicken Skewer

- Recipe ID: `chicken-skewer`
- Yield: Approx. 60–65 skewers from 5 kg chicken
- Label: Chicken Skewers
- Tags: House Standard \| Tapas \| Oven Roast \| Gluten-Free \| chicken \| dairy \| Weddings \| Parties \| Corporate \| Funeral & Wake \| Riviera Table / Offsite
- Package / section refs: Weddings: Portofino Package; Weddings: Taormina Package; Weddings: Amalfi Package; Weddings: La Tavola Package; Parties: Crowd Pleaser Platters; Corporate: Build-Your-Own Buffet; Funeral & Wake: Wake Platters; Riviera Table / Offsite: Canape Packages; Riviera Table / Offsite: BBQ Buffet; Riviera Table / Offsite: Plated Meals; Riviera Table / Offsite: Feasting — Shared to Table
- Menu description: Tzatziki Labneh · Crumbled Feta · Chargrilled Lemon
- Elements: Chicken Skewers \| Tzatziki Labneh \| Crumbled Feta \| Microherbs \| Chargrilled Lemon Cheek

#### Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient / prep |
| --- | --- |
| 5 kg | Chicken Thigh - diced into 2.5 cm pieces |
| 200 ml | Olive Oil |
| 150 g | Lemon Pepper Seasoning |
| 500 ml | Limoncello |
| 900 g | Lemons - approx. 6 lemons, juice and zest |
| 100 g | Honey or Castor Sugar |
| 25 g | Fresh Thyme - approx. 1 bunch, leaves picked |
| 500 g | Crumbled Feta - adjust to event size |
| 2 kg | Greek Yogurt - hung overnight for labneh |
| 600 g | Cucumber - approx. 2 large; deseeded and finely diced |
| 30 g | Dill - approx. 1 bunch, finely chopped |
| 30 g | Mint - approx. 1 bunch, finely chopped |
| 50 g | Parsley - approx. 1 bunch, finely chopped |
| 60 g | Microherbs - approx. 2 punnets |

#### Prep method

1. MARINADE: Combine olive oil, lemon pepper seasoning, limoncello, lemon juice and zest, honey or castor sugar and thyme in a large bowl.
2. CHICKEN: Dice chicken thigh into 2.5 cm pieces. Add to marinade and mix until fully coated. Refrigerate overnight if time allows.
3. SKEWER: Use 12 cm wooden skewers. Thread 3 pieces of chicken per skewer. Store skewers in 500 ml PCs, 3 skewers per serve. Label and freeze or refrigerate for service.
4. LABNEH: Line a perforated bain with 3 layers of chux, set inside a deep bain, add Greek yogurt, cover with more chux and leave overnight in the coldroom to hang.
5. TZATZIKI LABNEH: Finely chop parsley, dill and mint. Deseed and finely dice cucumber. Fold herbs, cucumber, lemon zest and lemon juice through the hung labneh. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
6. GARNISH: Trim microherbs with scissors, place in a labelled PC and refrigerate.

#### On the day / service

1. Defrost skewers in the coldroom for at least 24 hours if frozen.
2. Preheat oven to 180°C.
3. Lay skewers flat on trays without overlapping. Roast for about 10 minutes, or until golden and 75°C internal.
4. Plate a heaped tablespoon of tzatziki labneh in the centre of the plate. Place 3 skewers on top.
5. Finish with crumbled feta, microherbs and a chargrilled lemon cheek if required.

#### Notes

Use the new limoncello marinade and tzatziki labneh spec. Keep raw chicken fully submerged/coated in marinade before storage.

### Crispy Fried Chorizo Potatoes

- Recipe ID: `chorizo-potatoes`
- Yield: Approx. 60–65 canape portions
- Label: Chorizo Potatoes
- Tags: House Standard \| Canape \| Deep Fry \| Gluten-Free \| pork \| Weddings
- Package / section refs: Weddings: Portofino Package; Weddings: Taormina Package
- Menu description: Lemon Thyme Aioli · Shallots
- Elements: Potatoes \| Chorizo \| Lemon Thyme Aioli \| Shallots

#### Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient / prep |
| --- | --- |
| 10 kg | White Washed Potatoes |
| 2 kg | Chorizo - halved lengthwise, then sliced diagonally |
| 100 g | Shallots - approx. 1 bunch, thinly sliced |
| 600 g | Lemon Thyme Aioli - for tossing and finishing |
| as needed | Salt Flakes - to season after frying |
| as needed | Cracked Pepper - to finish |
| 50 | Small Bamboo Boats - or as required |

#### Prep method

1. POTATOES: Bake washed potatoes at 180°C for 35–40 minutes, or until soft all the way through. Cool.
2. SMASH: Pull cooled potatoes into uneven pieces, aiming for 6–8 pieces per large potato.
3. STORE: Place potato pieces in PCs, label and refrigerate.
4. CHORIZO: Slice chorizo lengthwise, then cut into 5 mm diagonal slices at a 45-degree angle.
5. SHALLOTS: Thinly slice shallots at a 45-degree angle. Store separately in a labelled PC.

#### On the day / service

1. Deep fry cooled potato pieces until crispy and golden.
2. Add chorizo slices to the fryer basket for the final 20–30 seconds.
3. Transfer fried potatoes and chorizo to a large metal bowl. Add Lemon Thyme Aioli and sliced shallots, then season to taste.
4. Toss so the aioli lightly coats the potatoes rather than pooling.
5. Serve 4–5 pieces in bamboo boats with a bamboo fork.

#### Notes

Aioli amount is kitchen-rounded for the batch because the DOCX lists it as an element without a quantity.

### Crispy Reef Fish Slider

- Recipe ID: `fish-slider`
- Yield: 50 sliders
- Label: Fish Slider
- Tags: House Standard \| Canape \| Deep Fry \| seafood \| Weddings
- Package / section refs: Weddings: Portofino Package
- Menu description: Lemon Caper Aioli · Roquette
- Elements: Reef Fish \| Lemon Caper Aioli \| Rocket \| Slider Buns

#### Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient / prep |
| --- | --- |
| 2.5 kg | Reef Fish - cut into 50 g pieces |
| 200 g | Lemon - juice and zest |
| 1 kg | Kewpie Mayo |
| 15 g | Dill — Fresh - approx. 1/2 bunch, finely chopped |
| 500 g | Rocket |
| 500 g | Baby Capers - drained |
| 1 kg | Self Raising Flour |
| 1 L | Sparkling Water - cold |
| 50 | Brioche Slider Buns |
| 50 g | Salt Flakes |
| 30 g | Cracked Pepper |
| 20 g | Garlic Powder |
| 20 g | Onion Powder |
| 30 ml | Vodka - optional crisping addition from method |

#### Prep method

1. FISH: Defrost fish in the coldroom 2 days before the event. The day before, cut into pieces approx. 50–70 g each and pat dry.
2. BATTER: Mix self raising flour, garlic powder, onion powder, salt and pepper. Add cold sparkling water and whisk to pancake-batter consistency. Add vodka last if using.
3. PART COOK: Preheat fryer. Dip fish into batter, shake off excess and fry in batches for about 1 minute, just long enough to seal the batter.
4. DRAIN & STORE: Drain part-cooked fish on a rack over paper towel. Place in a single layer in bains lined with baking paper. Wrap, label and refrigerate.
5. AIOLI: Drain capers and pat dry. Zest and juice lemon. Combine capers, lemon zest, lemon juice, Kewpie mayo, dill, salt and pepper. Label and refrigerate.
6. BUNS: Defrost brioche buns. Separate tops and bottoms into bains, wrap and store in fridge.

#### On the day / service

1. Warm brioche buns at 170°C for about 10 minutes.
2. Fry part-cooked fish until golden, crisp and hot through.
3. Place bun bases on a platter. Add a small handful of rocket to each base.
4. Place fried fish on rocket and spoon or pipe Lemon Caper Aioli on top.
5. Add bun tops and secure each slider with a skewer. Send immediately.

#### Notes

DOCX table gives 50 g fish portions; method allows 70 g. Use 50 g for 50-slider yield, 70 g for more substantial sliders.

### Lemon & Thyme Aioli

- Recipe ID: `lemon-thyme-aioli`
- Yield: Approx. 1.1 L batch
- Label: Lemon Thyme Aioli
- Tags: House Standard \| Sauce \| Sauce / Base \| Mix \| Gluten-Free
- Menu description: Kewpie Mayo · Lemon · Fresh Thyme
- Elements: Lemon Thyme Aioli

#### Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient / prep |
| --- | --- |
| 1 kg | Kewpie Mayonnaise |
| 450 g | Lemons - approx. 3 lemons, juice and zest |
| 25 g | Fresh Thyme - approx. 1 bunch, leaves picked |
| 20 g | Salt |
| 10 g | Cracked Pepper |

#### Prep method

1. Pick thyme leaves from stems and place in a bowl.

### Camembert, Pecan & Cranberry Cigars

- Recipe ID: `camembert-cigars`

- Package / section refs: Weddings: Portofino Package; Weddings: Taormina Package
- Menu description: Thyme Infused Honey · Microherbs
- Elements: Cigars \| Thyme Infused Honey \| Microherbs

#### Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient / prep |
| --- | --- |
| 620 g | Camembert |
| 300 g | Pecans |
| 300 g | Dried Cranberries |
| 25 g | Fresh Thyme - approx. 1 bunch |
| 300 g | Honey |
| 2 | Cinnamon Sticks |
| 30 g | Salt Flakes |
| 20 g | Cracked Pepper |
| 750 g | Filo Pastry - approx. 2 packets |
| 150 g | Butter - melted, for brushing filo |
| 50 g | Crushed Garlic |
| 60 g | Microherbs - approx. 2 punnets |
| 50 | Small Bamboo Boats |

#### Prep method

1. MELT BUTTER: Melt butter in a small pan if using butter to brush filo sheets.
2. CHEESE: Cut camembert into 10 cm long sticks, about 1 cm thick. Keep cold.
3. FILLING: Finely chop pecans and dried cranberries.
4. FILO: Place two sheets of filo on a clean surface. Brush the top sheet with melted butter if using. Cut into long strips.
5. ASSEMBLE: Place camembert on the narrow end of each filo strip. Add a teaspoon of pecan-cranberry mixture and a small amount of crushed garlic if using the DOCX spec.

4. Garnish with chopped pecans, cranberries, microherbs and edible flowers if available.

### Beef Kofta

- Recipe ID: `beef-kofta`
- Yield: 50 koftas
- Label: Beef Kofta
- Tags: House Standard \| Canape \| Oven Bake \| Dairy-Free \| beef \| Weddings
- Package / section refs: Weddings: Portofino Package
- Menu description: Spicy Capsicum · Pita · Rocket
- Elements: Beef Kofta \| Spicy Capsicum Sauce \| Rocket \| Pita Bread

#### Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient / prep |
| --- | --- |
| 4 kg | Beef Mince |
| 300 g | Brown Onion |
| 1.5 kg | Spicy Capsicum Dip |
| 50 | Mini Pita Breads |
| 100 g | Flat Leaf Parsley - approx. 2 bunches, chopped |
| 100 g | Garlic — Crushed |
| 60 g | Fresh Mint - approx. 2 bunches, chopped |
| 10 g | Ground Cumin |
| 10 g | Ground Coriander |
| 10 g | Ground Cinnamon |
| 50 g | Salt Flakes |
| 30 g | Cracked Pepper |
| 10 g | Paprika |
| 500 g | Rocket - approx. 4 bags |
| 50 | Large Bamboo Boats |

#### Prep method

1. AROMATICS: Finely dice and sauté brown onion and crushed garlic until translucent. Cool before mixing with mince.
2. MIX: Combine beef mince, browned onion and garlic, chopped mint, ground cumin, ground coriander, ground cinnamon, pepper, paprika and salt. Mix until fully combined.
3. TEST: Cook about 1 tablespoon of mixture and taste. Adjust seasoning before shaping.
4. SHAPE: Roll into 50 g balls, then shape each into a football/oval shape.
5. FREEZE: Lay koftas in a bain lined with baking paper. Cover and freeze until solid.
6. PACK: Once frozen, vacuum seal 40 koftas per bag. Label and return to freezer.
7. DEFROST: Remove from freezer at least 24 hours before use.
8. PITA: Defrost pita at least 24 hours before the event. Cut in half, line in a bain, cover, label and refrigerate.
9. GARNISH: Finely chop parsley and mint. Store in a labelled PC.

#### On the day / service

1. Cook koftas in a bain tray at 180°C for about 20 minutes, or until 75°C internal.
2. Warm pita bread at 170°C for about 10 minutes.
3. Open each pita and place into a large bamboo boat. Add rocket, then one kofta.
4. Top with spicy capsicum sauce and chopped herbs.
5. Arrange neatly on a platter and serve hot.

#### Notes

New DOCX spec uses 50 g koftas and keeps the sauce as spicy capsicum dip rather than a cooked sauce.
