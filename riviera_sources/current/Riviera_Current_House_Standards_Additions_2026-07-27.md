#### Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient / prep |
| --- | --- |
| 500 g | Canned Peaches - drained weight; drain 15 min, pat dry and cut to an even 5 mm dice |
| 10 ml | Wholegrain Seeded Mustard |

#### Prep method

1. DRAIN: Drain the peaches thoroughly for 15 min. Pat dry and cut into an even 5 mm dice. Keep chilled.
2. DRESSING: Whisk the seeded mustard, Tabasco, MSG, oregano, kaffir lime oil, lemon juice and black pepper until evenly combined. Fold through the capers and pickled cucumber.
3. FOLD: Fold the dressing gently through the peaches without crushing the dice.
4. REST: Rest for 5 min, stir once, then drain any excess liquor before service.
5. HOLD: Transfer to a labelled PC, cover and hold at 0–5°C. Use on the day of preparation. Do not add salt unless the final taste requires it; capers, pickle, mustard and MSG provide the standard seasoning.

#### On the day / service

1. Serve chilled as a cold condiment and portion to the relevant dish specification.
2. Before plating, stir once and drain any free liquid so the tartare holds a clean dice on the plate.
3. Keep covered at 0–5°C during service and use on the day of preparation.

#### Prep method

1. MIX: Combine 1080 g scone mix and 600 ml water. Mix only until combined; do not overmix.
2. REST: Cover and rest the dough for 5 minutes.
3. SHAPE: Turn onto a lightly floured bench. Handle as little as possible. Cut 12 normal scones or 40 small high-tea scones. Press the cutter straight down without twisting; re-pat scraps once only.
4. BAKE — STAGE 1: Bake at 200°C for 12 minutes.
5. BAKE — STAGE 2: Reduce to 160°C and bake for another 10 minutes. Cool briefly on a wire rack before packing.
6. CHANTILLY: Whip cold cream, icing sugar and vanilla in a chilled bowl to soft-firm peaks. Yield approximately 180 g chantilly per 12 regular scones.
7. SCONES BOX: Pack 12 regular scones with 120 g jam and chantilly. Pack cream and jam separately for delivery unless service is immediate.

#### On the day / service

1. Scones box = 12 regular scones with jam and cream.
2. Best served warm on the day of baking.
3. If made ahead, reheat at 160°C for 5–6 min — do not overheat or they dry out.
4. Pack cream and jam separately for delivery unless immediate service.

#### Prep method

2. FULL-GN FILO BASE: Build four bottom layers using seven filo sheets per layer, 28 sheets total. Butter every sheet and distribute pecans, walnuts, pistachios and cinnamon through the layers. Leave overhang for folding.
3. FILL & FOLD: Add the measured filling. Fold filo over the filling, buttering between every fold and overlap. Finish each full GN with one extra buttered top sheet: minimum 29 sheets per full GN plus spares.
4. HALF-GN: Build the half GN with the same butter-every-sheet principle. The exact sheet layout remains a confirmation item; do not present an inferred count as locked.
5. BAKE: Rational hot air 155°C, 0% steam, low fan. Start checking at 40 minutes. The exterior should be set and the centre should retain a slow, shallow wobble.
6. REST & CHILL: Turn the oven off and leave the door cracked for 15–20 minutes. Cool safely, then chill overnight before portioning.
7. SYRUP: Bring water, sugar, honey, lemon juice and cinnamon sticks to the boil, then simmer until lightly syrupy. Remove from heat, remove cinnamon and stir in rose water. Allocate two-thirds to the trays and chill one-third as the service reserve.

#### On the day / service

#### Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient / prep |
| --- | --- |
| 2.5 kg | Pizza Flour |
| 2 L | Water - warm (35–40°C) |
| 15 g | Dry Yeast |
| 50 g | Table Salt |
| 50 ml | Tuscan Oil - plus extra for drizzling |
| 20 ml | Pure Honey |

#### Prep method

1. Dissolve dry yeast in warm water with honey. Stand 5 minutes until frothy.
2. Combine pizza flour and salt in a large mixing bowl or commercial mixer. Add yeast mixture and Tuscan Oil. Mix on low speed until a shaggy dough forms, then on medium for 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
3. Transfer to an oiled container. Cover and prove at room temperature for 60–90 minutes until doubled.
4. Generously oil two deep oven trays (approximately 60 × 40 cm each). Divide dough equally and press out to fill each tray. Dimple aggressively with fingers all over the surface.
5. Cover loosely and prove a further 45–60 minutes until puffy and jiggly.
6. Before baking, drizzle generously with Tuscan Oil and season with flaked salt. Press in red grapes or rosemary for the house version.
7. Bake at 220°C (conventional) for 20–25 minutes until deep golden brown and the base sounds hollow when tapped. Cool on a rack.

#### On the day / service

1. Cut into portions approximately 6–8 cm × 4–5 cm.
2. Serve warm or at room temperature — reheat at 180°C for 5 minutes if pre-baked.
3. For function bread service: basket-lined portions on the table before the entrée.
4. Casual delivery or grazing box: 12 pieces. Every grazing box includes focaccia.
5. Grazing table: 1 m uses 1/4 tray; 2 m uses 1/2 tray.
6. Include for plated dinners and non-DIY/MYO buffets. Do not include automatically for DIY/MYO packages.

#### Ingredients

| Qty | Ingredient / prep |
| --- | --- |
| 780 g | Instant Mash - potato flakes |
| 3.5 L | Water - boiling |
| 1.5 L | Thickened Cream - heated |
| 1 kg | Butter Unsalted - cubed |
| 1 pkt | Thyme - leaves stripped |
| 25 g | Table Salt |
| 5 g | Ground Black Pepper |

#### Prep method

1. Combine boiling water and hot thickened cream in a large bowl. Add potato flakes and mix well until absorbed and lump-free. Cover and rest 2 minutes.
2. In a heavy-based saucepan, melt butter over medium-high heat. Cook until the milk solids turn golden brown and smell nutty (beurre noisette stage), approximately 4–5 minutes. Watch closely and remove from heat immediately.
3. Pour the browned butter over the mash — include the brown solids. Add thyme leaves, salt and pepper. Mix vigorously until smooth and silky.
4. Taste and adjust seasoning. Mash should be rich, creamy and well-seasoned.
5. Transfer to a deep hotel pan. Cover surface directly with cling film to prevent a skin. Hold in a bain marie at 75°C, or refrigerate and reheat.

#### On the day / service

1. Serve in a bain marie at 75°C or pipe/quenelle per plate.
2. For plated: portion 150–180 g per plate using a piping bag or large spoon.
3. For buffet: serve from hotel pan. Stir every 20 minutes and top with a small knob of butter.
4. Holds 4 days refrigerated. Reheat gently with a splash of cream, stirring constantly.
