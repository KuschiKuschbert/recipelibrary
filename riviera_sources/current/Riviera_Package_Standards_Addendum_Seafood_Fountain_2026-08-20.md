## Seafood fountain count standard

Use the confirmed guest count and selected fountain format:

- **Oyster-only:** 1 oyster per person.
- **Prawn-only:** 3 prawns per person.
- **Mixed:** 1 oyster and 3 prawns per person.

This is an exact fixed package module. Do not apply an automatic 9% event buffer unless the confirmed event source explicitly overrides the module. Convert the exact requirement to current supplier packs and show unavoidable pack over-order separately.

## Still requires event or sales confirmation

- Final fountain selection
- Current price and minimum
- Setup fee and bond
- Seafood supplier availability and delivery timing
- Food-safe ice, display footprint and drainage
- Mollusc/crustacean allergen and cross-contact controls
